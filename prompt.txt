ScholarAI - Research Paper Drafting Assistant

Platform: Google AI Studio
Prompting method: Few-shot prompting

Goal:
Create a student-friendly research paper assistant
that generates structured academic drafts from
a research topic.

Required sections:
1. Research title
2. Abstract
3. Introduction
4. Literature review guidance
5. Research objectives
6. Methodology suggestions
7. Conclusion

Few-shot example 1:
Input: Artificial Intelligence in Education
Expected output: A structured academic research
draft about AI applications in education.

Few-shot example 2:
Input: Renewable Energy in Smart Cities
Expected output: A structured academic research
draft about renewable energy integration
in smart cities.

For new topics, follow the same academic structure.
Do not invent references, citations,
or research results.

Current implementation:
Offline template-based draft generation.
